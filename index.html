Ratatouille (Confit Byaldi)

The movie Ratatouille (2007) from Pixar Animation Studios changes what most think ratatouille is. The dish served as ratatouille in the movie is actually something called Confit Byaldi. This dish is made by layering tomatoes, green squash, eggplant, and yellow squash baked in a a pipérade sauce.
