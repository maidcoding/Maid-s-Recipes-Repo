Ratatouille (Confit Byaldi)

There's two main parts to the ratatouille, they are the squashes and the sauce. No part of this recipe is particularly hard, however the preparation of it can be time consuming.

Equipment Needed

  • Knife
  • Cutting Board
  • Oven
  • Oven Safe Pan
  • Blender
  • Open Flame If Available

Ingredients

  • 2 Green Squash (Italian Squash)
  • 2 Yellow Squash
  • 1 Eggplant
  • 4-5 Tomatoes
  • 1/2 An Onion
  • 3-4 Bellpeppers
  • 4 Cloves of Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Step 1: The Sauce (Pipérade)

For the sauce we'll need to skin a few tomatoes, roast some bellpeppers, saute a few cloves of garlic, and an onion, and blend is all together with olive oil, and finally cook it to bring all the flavors together.

  1. Peel the tomatoes

    2. To peel the tomatoes you will need to boil a pot of water. Then, cut an X into the bottom of it going up it's sides.You then put the tomatoes in the pot for around three minutes, let them cool down so you can handle then, and use the heel of your knife to lift each corner of the skin, and pull the skin off. Finally you need to cut out where the stem attaches, and put them to the side for later.

  2. Roast the bellpeppers

    3. Place your bellpeppers over an open flame, and once you see some charring on the skin of it, rotate it to char the entire surface of each pepper. Once each bellpepper is charred put them in a large bowl with aluminum foil covering it. About 5 minutes after you finish the final one, (to let them cool down, and steam each other) use a clean kitchen rag to rub off some of the charred skin and put them back in the bowl for later.

  3. Saute the onion

    4. Roughly chop half an onion, and saute it in olive oil over medium heat until they start to turn translucent and soften.

  4. Saute the garlic

    5. Peel and roughly chop 4 cloves of garlic, and saute it in the same olive oil we used for the onions over medium heat for 3-5 minutes.

  5. Blend it all together

    6. Next, you'll need to put ONLY 2 or 3 of the skinned tomatoes depending on their size, the bellpeppers, onions, and garlic into the blender. Make sure everything isn't too hot before blending it otherwise the steam can pressurize the blender making a mess when you open the blender. Once everything is blended you'll start to slowly add the olive oil you used to saute the onions and garlic in into the blender until it emulsifies.

  6. Cook the sauce

    7. Finally for the sauce you need to pour the contents of the blender into a pot, and let it cook at low to medium-low heat. As you start the next part, make sure to stir the sauce ever 5 to 7 minutes to prevent any burning on the bottom of the pot.

Step 2: Cutting The Vegetables

  1. Thinly slice the vegetables

    2. For the squashes, eggplant, and tomatoes, you will need to slice all of them to around a 1/4 inch to an 1/8 inch thick. Do your best to cut them all as evenly as possible so everything bakes evenly. If you have a mandoline this would be a perfect use for it. This step and the next are the easiest to follow along with, but the most time consuming.

Step 3: Time to Bake :)

  1. Pre-heat the oven to 375° F
  2. Take your sauce off the heat and add it into your baking dish.
  3. Layer your vegetables in a pattern like, green squash, tomato, yellow squash, eggplant, or any variation of that, and repeat.
  4. Once the pan is full and your oven is up to temperature put it on the top rack of the oven.
  5. Bake the ratatouille for 35-45 minutes, checking with a fork to see if it's cooked and tender.

Optional steps.

  • You can skip peeling the tomatoes for the sauce if you want to instead use a can of crushed tomatoes.
  • While cooking the sauce you can add some herbs to it like rosemary, thyme, or oregano.
  • During the last 15 minutes of baking I'd recommend adding some cheese on top.
